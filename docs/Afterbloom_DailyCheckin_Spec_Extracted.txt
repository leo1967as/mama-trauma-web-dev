4.1   Scoring  Logic  
mood_score Raw  score   1–5   (1  =  worst)  
sleep_score Raw  score   1–5   (1  =  worst)  
energy_score Raw  score   1–5   (1  =  worst)  
worry_score  (inverted) 
adjusted_worry  =  6  -  worry_score   (so  1  =  best,  5  =  worst)  
composite_score (mood_score  +  sleep_score  +  energy_score  +  adjusted_worry)  /  4  
composite  range 1.0  =  all  worst   |   5.0  =  all  best  
Override  rule If  support_request  was  triggered  and  baby_connection_score  =  1:  escalate  to  Level  3  regardless  of  composite  
 
4.2   Support  Level  Definitions   
Level  1   Steady 
Trigger  condition composite_score  >=  3.5  
Message  to  mother 
You  seem  steady  today.  Keep  going  at  your  own  pace.  
CTA  buttons →   View  today's  care  tip  →   See  your  mood  trend  
Research  basis Positive  reinforcement  increases  sustained  engagement  (JMIR  Human  Factors,  MBapp  2024).  Steady  days  should  feel  rewarding,  not  neutral. 
 
Level  2   Gentle  Support 
Trigger  condition composite_score  >=  2.5  AND  <  3.5  Message  to  mother 
Today  might  feel  a  little  heavy.  You  don't  have  to  push  through  everything  at  once.  
CTA  buttons →   Choose  one  small  goal  →   Breathing  exercise  
Research  basis Behavioral  activation  (CBT  micro-actions)  for  mild  symptoms  has  evidence  in  MBapp  2024.  Small  achievable  actions  reduce  learned  helplessness. 
 
Level  3   Extra  Support  Recommended 
Trigger  condition composite_score  >=  1.5  AND  <  2.5   |   OR   |   mood_score  <=  2  AND  adjusted_worry  >=  4  
Message  to  mother 
You  may  need  a  little  more  support  right  now.  Afterbloom  recommends  an  emotional  check-in  or  talking  to  someone  you  trust.  
CTA  buttons →   Take  emotional  screening  (EPDS)  →   Talk  to  your  support  circle  →   Contact  your  hospital  team  
Research  basis EMA  pilot  2022:  3-day  low  trend  has  predictive  validity  for  EPDS  high  score.  Level  3  triggers  EPDS  screening  prompt. 
 
Level  4   Immediate  Support 
Trigger  condition support_request  =  true   (mother  tapped  'Yes,  please'  on  Support  Need  question)  
Message  to  mother 
You  don't  have  to  go  through  this  alone.  We'll  connect  you  with  support  right  away.  
CTA  buttons →   Contact  hospital  team  →   Call  emergency  line  →   I'm  with  someone  safe  
Research  basis Triggered  by  mother's  explicit  request  for  help.  Hospital  dashboard  receives  alert.  ACOG  2023:  hospital  follow-up  is  mandatory  when  support  need  is  flagged.
